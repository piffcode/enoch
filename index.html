Why we're doing it like this

We wanted to keep this simple and intentional. At the end of the day, all a baby truly needs is love — and Enoch is already surrounded by so much of it. Instead of accumulating items all at once, we're choosing to make thoughtful purchases as we go, based on what he actually needs.

Your flexible support allows us to do that with care and without excess. No pressure at all — your love and presence in our lives already mean everything.
