Why we’re doing it like this

We wanted to keep this really simple and intentional.

We don’t want a lot of things — because at the end of the day, all a baby truly needs is love. And Enoch is already surrounded by so much of it.

Instead of receiving a lot of items all at once, we’re choosing to make thoughtful purchases as we go — based on what Enoch actually needs, when he needs it.

Support that’s flexible allows us to do that with care and without excess. No pressure at all — your love and presence in our lives already mean everything.
